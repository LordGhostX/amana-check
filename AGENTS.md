# Writing style for this repo

Everything outside the block above is ours. Write docs, UI copy, and commit messages in plain English. The rules come from Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing.

- Prefer plain verbs. Use is, are, and has. Avoid "serves as", "stands as", "represents", "plays a key role".
- Avoid the AI vocabulary: delve, robust, crucial, pivotal, seamless, leverage, harness, foster, showcase, underscore, testament, tapestry, landscape, realm, meticulous, intricate, profound, myriad, plethora, valuable, vibrant, unlock, elevate. Dense clusters of these are the strongest tell.
- No negative parallelism: not just X but Y, not X but Y, Y rather than X as a rhetorical move.
- No decorative rule of three, meaning no strings of three adjectives or phrases added for rhythm.
- No present-participle commentary: highlighting, underscoring, emphasizing, reflecting, ensuring.
- Em dashes are rare. Use commas, colons, or parentheses. Straight quotes and apostrophes only.
- No bold lead-in bullets like "**Label.** description". Use a plain list or prose.
- Keep specific numbers, dates, names, file paths, and the reason a choice was made. These are signs of human writing.
- Do not add chatbot framing: "I hope this helps", "let me know", disclaimers about knowledge cutoffs, or placeholder text.
- Headings are sentence case and do not repeat the document title.
