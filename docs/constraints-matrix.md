# Amana Check: constraints matrix

The OSF × Andela brief defines seven operating constraints. This matrix maps each one to what is built, where it lives, and how the demo shows it.

| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Trust and verification           | Sources are tiered (T1 official, T2 independent, T3 media). Answers carry citations, publisher, publication date, and the time of the check. Corrections are versioned. "Unknown" is a valid status, and a verified verdict is capped at developing when the claim names a place that no source mentions. | `src/lib/trust/freshness.ts`, `src/lib/pipeline/synthesize.ts`, `src/lib/db/schema.ts`            | The answer card shows the status, cited bullets, and source excerpts. ## How the demo covers the seven constraints in one interaction

A forwarded message pasted on the home page produces an answer card with the detected language, a status and reason, a source list with dates, plain-language bullets, a reply in the user's language, location-aware evidence, and contact next steps. Privacy and low bandwidth are structural rather than visible on screen, and they are documented in this file, the methodology, and the threat model.
